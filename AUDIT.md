# drillmaster.sk — technical SEO audit

Audited live on **6 Aug 2026** by crawling the site directly (headers, rendered
DOM, sitemap, schema, HTTP status of every linked URL).

The headline: the current site is **better than it looks** — it already has
schema, canonicals and real 350–450-word city pages. The problems are not
"the site is bad", they are **structural**: a broken duplicate URL tree, and
half the service×city matrix simply never built.

---

## 1. Broken pages linked from the homepage — fix first

The homepage "Naše pobočky" block links to `/lokality/{mesto}/`. Six of those
seven URLs are **soft 404s**: they return HTTP 200, but render a 404 heading,
carry ~89 words, and set `canonical` to the homepage.

| URL | Status | Words | Canonical |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/lokality/zvolen/` | real page | 230 | **none** |
| `/lokality/bratislava/` | **soft 404** | 89 | → homepage |
| `/lokality/banska-bystrica/` | **soft 404** | 89 | → homepage |
| `/lokality/brezno/` | **soft 404** | 89 | → homepage |
| `/lokality/lucenec/` | **soft 404** | 89 | → homepage |
| `/lokality/nitra/` | **soft 404** | 89 | → homepage |
| `/lokality/ruzomberok/` | **soft 404** | 89 | → homepage |

Why it matters: the homepage is the strongest page on the domain, and it is
currently spending six internal links on dead ends. Soft 404s waste crawl
budget and look like low-quality content to Google.

**Fix:** 301 them into the real `/jadrove-vrtanie/{mesto}/` pages. Already
implemented in `next.config.mjs` → `redirects()`.

---

## 2. Duplicate city pages competing for the same keyword

Two separate pages target *jadrové vŕtanie Zvolen*:

| | `/lokality/zvolen/` | `/jadrove-vrtanie/zvolen/` |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Jadrové vŕtanie a rezanie betónu Zvolen | Jadrové vŕtanie Zvolen \| Vŕtanie prestupov |
| Words | 230 | 446 |
| Canonical | **none** | correct (self) |
| Schema | 1 block | 1 block |

Two URLs chasing one query splits link equity and lets Google pick the wrong
one. The `/jadrove-vrtanie/` version is the stronger page and should win —
hence the redirect direction above.

---

## 3. The biggest opportunity: half the matrix doesn't exist

The site sells **two** services and lists **seven** branches — that's 14
possible service×city pages. Today:

- `/jadrove-vrtanie/{mesto}/` — exists for 6 of 7 (**Lučenec missing**)
- `/rezanie-otvorov/{mesto}/` — **exists for 0 of 7**

So **8 of 14 money pages are unbuilt**, including every single
"rezanie otvorov {mesto}" page. Nothing on the site currently targets
*rezanie panelu v byte Bratislava*, *rezanie otvorov Nitra*, and so on —
despite rezanie being half the business.

This is the single highest-return item in the rebuild, and the new codebase
generates these pages automatically once the per-city content is written.

---

## 4. Sitemap is stale and incomplete

`/sitemap.xml` was hand-generated by `xml-sitemaps.com` (its comment is still
in the file). It lists 12 URLs, all with `lastmod` frozen at 2026-05-10, and:

- includes the duplicate `/lokality/zvolen/`
- **omits** `/jadrove-vrtanie/lucenec/`
- omits all other `/lokality/*` URLs (which is right, but inconsistent)

**Fix:** the rebuild generates `sitemap.xml` from the same data that generates
the pages (`app/sitemap.ts`), so it can never drift again.

---

## 5. What the current site already does well — keep it

Do not throw these away in the rebuild:

- **`HomeAndConstructionBusiness` schema** with a stable `@id`, and `hasMap`
  pointing at the Google Business Profile (`cid=7556354895049639611`). That GBP
  link is valuable — the rebuild preserves both.
- **Self-referencing canonicals** on the service and `/jadrove-vrtanie/*` pages.
- **Genuinely localised city copy** — the Zvolen page really does talk about
  Zvolen rather than swapping a name into a template. That's the standard the
  new city pages are held to.
- **Clean URL slugs** in Slovak, with trailing slashes, already keyword-aligned.

---

## Priority order

1. **301 the `/lokality/*` soft 404s** → real service pages *(done in config)*
2. **Build the 7 `/rezanie-otvorov/{mesto}/` pages** — biggest new traffic
3. **Add `/jadrove-vrtanie/lucenec/`** — listed as a branch, has no page
4. **Auto-generate the sitemap** *(done — `app/sitemap.ts`)*
5. Write unique content for the 5 unpublished cities in `data/cities.ts`
6. Verify every page in Google's Rich Results Test after deploy
7. Submit the new sitemap in Search Console and watch the coverage report

---

## Caveat worth stating plainly

Everything here is on-page. For a local trade business, the **Google Business
Profile** (reviews, photos, categories, service areas) usually drives more
calls than the website does for "jadrové vŕtanie {mesto}" searches. The site's
job is to rank in the organic results below the map pack and convert. Treat
the rebuild as one half of the work, not the whole of it.
